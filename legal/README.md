# Legal templates

Trust agreements attached to Digital Deeds, by region. Each Digital Deed is governed by the trust agreement attached to it when it was minted.

## United States

| File | Status |
| --- | --- |
| `Americas/US/trust-agreement.md` | Current template for existing Digital Deeds |
| `Americas/US/real-property-trust-agreement-v1-draft.md` | Draft v1.0 for comment; awaiting counsel review; not for use |
| `Americas/US/vehicles-and-equipment-trust-agreement-v1-draft.md` | Draft v1.0 for comment; awaiting counsel review; not for use |

The adopted version of each v1.0 template will be published with its content hash.

## Other regions

Canada, Mexico, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Russia, Indonesia and Japan each have a `trust-agreement.md` template in their folder.

See `CHANGELOG.md` for changes. Nothing here is legal advice; see `../DISCLAIMER.md`.
