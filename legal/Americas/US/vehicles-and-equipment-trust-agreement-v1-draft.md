This Trust Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into by the Grantor through the creation of a Deed Token on the Deed Protocol. The identifying information of this trust (the "**Trust**") may be found in the Deed Token to which this Agreement is attached.

### How This Trust Works (Non-Operative Summary)

*This summary is provided for the reader's convenience only. It is not an operative part of this Agreement, creates no rights or obligations, and shall not be used to construe any operative provision. If this summary and any operative provision differ, the operative provision controls.*

- A trust holds title to one specific vehicle, state-titled vessel, trailer, unaffixed manufactured or relocatable home, or piece of equipment (the "Asset"). Vessels documented with the U.S. Coast Guard are excluded. For titled assets, the trustee is the owner shown on the state certificate of title. For untitled equipment, the trustee holds title by bill of sale.
- The entire beneficial interest in the trust is personal property, and it belongs to whoever rightfully holds the Deed Token. Transfer the token and ownership of the trust passes with it. The certificate of title does not change when the token moves.
- The token holder has possession and use of the Asset, keeps any income from it, and is responsible for everything that comes with operating it: registration, taxes, insurance, maintenance, tickets, and liability to others. The trustee never possesses or operates the Asset.
- The trustee is a title-holder only: it acts solely on instruction, never possesses, operates, or manages the Asset, and never handles money. Unless another qualified trustee is named, the owner is its own trustee (an "owner-trustee") from the start, and the office passes to each new owner with the token; an owner may instead ask for a licensed trust company or nonprofit trustee, which only the owner may pay. A program sponsor, and each entity it wholly owns, holds title to its own assets directly as owner-trustee; a program's title administrator never holds title. A trust protector — the owner by default, or a program sponsor such as a fleet operator — can replace the trustee. Neither can move the token or change who owns it.
- If the owner lets required insurance lapse and does not cure it, the trustee — acting on the standing instruction every owner gives by accepting the trust — takes the Asset out of the trust and titles it directly in the owner's name. That protects the trustee from liability for a vehicle it does not control; it does not take anything away from the owner.
- Borrowing works in two lanes. A lender can take the token (a personal-property loan against the beneficial interest). A lender that wants a lien on the vehicle itself must be shown on the certificate of title, because state title law requires it. Lenders who need protection against the physical Asset take both.
- Equipment has no title registry, so whoever physically holds it can sometimes sell it to an innocent buyer. This Agreement uses identification plates and public notice filings to reduce that risk, but it cannot remove it.
- If the token keys are lost, a sworn, notarized claim, onchain notice, and a 90-day contest period let the owner take the Asset out of the trust. The trustee and protector are told of every claim; the protector can contest it, and the trustee will not act on a claim it has reason to believe is false.
- If the Asset is destroyed, repossessed by a lienholder of record, sold for unpaid charges, or seized, the trust ends, the token becomes void, and the token holder keeps the right to any proceeds.
- If a relocatable home or other Asset becomes real property under state law, the owner can move the trust to the real-property agreement.
- Wyoming law governs the trust. California's version of UCC Article 12 governs the token as a digital record. State title law and the law where the Asset is located govern the Asset itself.

### Recitals

- Whereas blockchain Smart Contracts provide a secure and interoperable way of representing ownership of assets through an electronic ledger that tracks, documents, and verifies transactions.
- Whereas the Grantor wishes to link ownership of the Asset to a Deed Token, and intends this Agreement to act as the trust agreement governing the trust that holds the Asset.
- Whereas the parties intend that the entire beneficial interest in the Trust is personal property, freely assignable by the digital mechanics of this Agreement, while title to the Asset itself is held, transferred, and encumbered only under the certificate-of-title law, commercial law, and other law that govern the Asset.

### 1. Trust Purpose

1. The purpose of the Trust is to create a legally enforceable link such that ownership of the Deed Token determines beneficial ownership of the Asset, so as to allow for efficient, accurate, and secure ownership, transfer, use, and financing of the Asset in the digital environment (the "**Trust Purpose**").
2. On creation, the Trust takes and holds title to the Asset, and ownership of the Trust is represented by ownership of the Deed Token. Regardless of any sale, pledge, or other transfer of the Deed Token, title to the Asset remains in the Trust until either the then-present Beneficiary distributes the Asset out of the Trust, or title passes out of the Trust by an Operative Event (**Section 10.5**) or a Protective Distribution (**Section 9.5**). The Trustee shall not execute or deliver any assignment of title, bill of sale, or other instrument transferring the Asset out of the Trust unless and until (a) the Deed Token has been Redeemed, or (b) the transfer is authorized under **Section 9.5, 10.2, 10.3, 10.4, or 10.9**.
3. Subject to **Sections 6 and 7**, the owner of the Account or Smart Wallet rightfully holding the Deed Token is the Beneficiary of the Trust (the office of Trustee being governed by **Section 13**).
4. **Nature of the Beneficial Interest.** The entire beneficial interest in the Trust — comprising the Owner Rights of the present Beneficiary and every contingent interest of the other Beneficial Interest Holders (collectively, the "**Beneficial Interest**") — is, and is declared by the Grantor and agreed by every person who at any time claims under this Agreement or the Deed Token to be, intangible personal property, distinct from the Asset itself. Accordingly: (a) the Beneficial Interest passes by assignment under **Section 7** upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership, without any assignment of the certificate of title or bill of sale; (b) a security interest in the Deed Token or the Beneficial Interest attaches, is perfected, has priority, and is enforced under UCC Article 9 as described in **Section 11**, and is not a security interest in the Asset; and (c) succession to the Beneficial Interest on the death of a Beneficiary is governed by the law governing intangible personal property and by **Section 7.6**.
5. **Integrity of the Trust Corpus.** The corpus of the Trust is fixed at its creation: it consists of the Asset described in the Deed Metadata at that time, together with its accessions, parts and accessories installed in the ordinary course of maintenance or repair, and the proceeds arising from it, and of nothing else. No direction of any Beneficiary, act of the Trustee, or instrument of any third party may substitute another asset for the corpus or add an asset to it, and the Trustee shall refuse, and has no power to accept, any purported addition. Any ambiguity concerning the composition of the corpus shall be resolved in favor of this principle. The only exceptions are the events described in **Sections 9.5, 10.5, and 10.9**.

### 2. Definitions

1. **Account** means an externally owned account on a Blockchain Network: an Address controlled directly by one or more private keys.
2. **Address** means a public key address on a Blockchain Network.
3. **Asset** means the single item of goods identified in the Deed Metadata under `definition.claim` — by vehicle identification number, hull identification number, serial number, or other unique identifier — that is transferred into the Trust: a Titled Asset or an item of Untitled Equipment. An Asset is goods; this Agreement does not apply to real property, to fixtures, or to any other kind of asset, except as **Section 10.9** provides. A vessel that is documented, or is required to be documented, under 46 U.S.C. chapter 121 (a "**Documented Vessel**") is not an Asset, and no Deed Token may be Minted for it.
4. **Beneficiary** means the person or persons holding present beneficial ownership of the Trust and the Owner Rights as determined under **Section 7**. Beneficial ownership is ordinarily established by control of the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Deed Token, but may differ from the onchain holder as **Sections 7.3, 7.5, 7.6, 7.7, and 11.3** provide. Before the Deed Token is first held by any Account, the Beneficiary is the Grantor. Where ownership of the Deed Token is fractionalized, "Beneficiary" refers to the fractional owners as a group.
5. **Beneficial Interest Holders** means the present Beneficiary and every person who would become a Beneficiary under the objective transfer and succession mechanics of **Sections 7 and 10.3**. The interests of persons other than the present Beneficiary are defeasible contingent equitable interests that confer no present right of action, possession, or control.
6. **Blockchain Network** means any distributed ledger network used to maintain records of ownership of the Deed Token, including any network to which the Deed Token is wrapped or migrated.
7. **Certificate of Title** means a certificate of title, registration record, or equivalent ownership record issued for a Titled Asset by a Titling Authority.
8. **Confirmed Transaction** means a transaction that has been recorded on the Blockchain Network and has become final under its consensus and settlement rules. A transaction on Ethereum is final when it becomes part of a block on the chain with a supermajority link between two checkpoint blocks. Where the Blockchain Network publishes its transaction data to a Settlement Layer for finality, a transaction is a Confirmed Transaction only when the transaction data containing it has been included in a block of the Settlement Layer that is itself final. A transaction that has only been accepted, ordered, or reported by a sequencer is not a Confirmed Transaction.
9. **Contract Account** means an Address controlled by a Smart Contract, operating either as a **Smart Wallet** (a custody solution directly controlled by specific persons, who thereby become Beneficiaries) or as a **Functional Contract** (an escrow, marketplace settlement contract, lending pool, bridge, wrapper, fractionalization mechanism, or similar utility, custody by which does not of itself confer beneficial ownership).
10. **Deed Contract** means the Smart Contract used to maintain records of ownership of the Deed Token, and any successor contract under **Section 14.3.4**.
11. **Deed ID** means the unique and immutable identifier assigned to the Deed Token on creation of the Trust, generated from fields of the Deed Metadata that include a cryptographic hash of the precise language of this Agreement and of the parameters stated at creation. A Deed ID shall never be assigned to any other Token, including after the Deed Token has been Redeemed or has become a Void Token.
12. **Deed Metadata** means the data stored within the Deed Token and the data stored on IPFS (or a similar immutable storage system) and linked within it, including the fields `definition` and `configuration` and onchain traits that the Deed Contract reserves against change except as this Agreement provides. The Deed Metadata always includes the Deed ID, the Trust Name (`definition.holdingEntity`), the identification of the Asset (`definition.claim`), the Asset Class, the Titling Authority and Certificate of Title number (for a Titled Asset), the Trustee, any Standby Trustee, the Trust Protector (or that the Beneficiary is Trust Protector), the content hash of this Agreement, and every parameter stated under **Section 14.1**.
13. **Deed Protocol** means the open-source system of Smart Contracts — including the Deed Contract and the Validator Registry — together with the agreements published for use with it.
14. **Deed Token** means the Token issued through the Deed Contract to which this Agreement is attached, being, if the Token is wrapped or transferred to another Blockchain Network, the ultimate controlling Token at any present moment.
15. **Lien of Record** means a security interest or lien in the Asset that is noted on the Certificate of Title (for a Titled Asset), or that is perfected under applicable law by filing, possession, or otherwise (for Untitled Equipment).
16. **Mint** means a Confirmed Transaction executing the issuance function of the Deed Contract that associates a Deed ID with an Address.
17. **Owner Rights** has the meaning given in **Section 6.1**.
18. **Qualified Trustee** means a person that (a) is one of the following: (i) a Qualified Validator; (ii) a trust company licensed or otherwise authorized to engage in the trust business under the law of the jurisdiction of the Titling Authority and of the jurisdiction where the Asset is principally located; (iii) a nonprofit entity that the law of those jurisdictions permits to act as trustee incidentally to its purposes; or (iv) the Beneficiary, serving as Owner-Trustee under **Section 13.2.3**; (b) is permitted by applicable law to hold title to the Asset as trustee on the terms of this Agreement, and to be shown as owner on the Certificate of Title, holding every license, registration, or bond that law requires of it for that service; (c) has accepted the office of Trustee by a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**; and (d) except for an Owner-Trustee, does not hold any part of the present Beneficial Interest.
19. **Redeem** means a Confirmed Transaction executing the retirement function of the Deed Contract that permanently locks or removes the Deed Token from circulation, so that it becomes untransferable forever.
20. **Settlement Layer** means Ethereum, or any other Blockchain Network to which the Blockchain Network on which the Deed Token is recorded publishes its transaction data for finality.
21. **Smart Contract** means code deployed at an Address on a Blockchain Network that executes a series of processes or interactions.
22. **Titled Asset** means an Asset for which a certificate-of-title statute of a state of the United States provides for the issuance of a certificate of title — including a motor vehicle, trailer, vessel, and a manufactured or relocatable home that has not become real property under applicable law — and **Titling Authority** means the agency that issues the Certificate of Title.
23. **Token** means a digital asset tracked by the Deed Contract under a unique identifier, implemented under the ERC-721 standard or any other standard used by the Deed Contract.
24. **Transfer** means any operation performed through the Deed Contract that assigns the Deed Token to a new non-null Address.
25. **Trust Name** means the name of the Trust stated in `definition.holdingEntity`, which should encode the Deed ID.
26. **Trust Protector** means the person determined under **Section 13.4.1**.
27. **Trustee** means the Qualified Trustee serving as trustee of the Trust under **Section 13**, or its successor.
28. **Untitled Equipment** means an Asset that is not a Titled Asset, including machinery, tools, electronic equipment, and other goods.
29. **Validator Registry** means the onchain registry of validators maintained as part of the Deed Protocol and identified by the Deed Contract.
30. **Void Token** means a Deed Token that has been dissociated from the Asset and the Trust under **Section 10**. A Void Token confers no beneficial ownership and no Owner Rights, except the wind-up rights and reinstatement eligibility that **Section 10.5** provides.
31. **Asset Class** means whether the Asset is a Titled Asset or Untitled Equipment, and its type (such as motor vehicle, trailer, vessel, manufactured or relocatable home, or equipment), as stated in the Deed Metadata.
32. **Program**, **Program Sponsor**, **Participation Agreement**, and **Eligible Transferee** have the meanings given in **Section 16**.
33. **Qualified Validator** means a person that is registered and active as a validator in the Validator Registry.
34. **Licensing Determination** means a written determination, delivered to the Trustee and the Trust Protector, by a regulator having jurisdiction over trust business where the Asset is titled or principally located, by a court of competent jurisdiction, or by counsel to the Trustee, the Trust Protector, or a Program Sponsor, that the Trustee may not lawfully serve, or continue to serve, as trustee of the Asset on the terms of this Agreement.
35. **Standby Trustee** means a Qualified Trustee of a kind described in clause (a)(ii) or (a)(iii) of **Section 2.18** that is identified in the Deed Metadata, or designated by the Trust Protector, and that has accepted in advance, by a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**, appointment as successor Trustee upon a Licensing Determination.
36. **Owner-Trustee** has the meaning given in **Section 13.2.3**.

### 3. Establishing the Trust

1. The process for entering into this Agreement is as follows:
   1. the Grantor, or a third party instructed by the Grantor, generates — and, where the Deed Contract provides for it, reserves — a Deed ID through the Deed Contract;
   2. the Trustee accepts its office under **Section 13.1.2**;
   3. the Grantor transfers title to the Asset into the Trust: (a) for a Titled Asset, by assigning the Certificate of Title and applying to the Titling Authority for a new Certificate of Title showing the Trustee, as trustee of the Trust (using the Trust Name), as owner, and — where the Titling Authority's forms permit — the Deed ID, together with every odometer, lien, and other disclosure the Titling Authority requires; and (b) for Untitled Equipment, by a bill of sale to the Trustee, as trustee of the Trust, that identifies the Asset and recites the Deed ID;
   4. the Grantor, or a third party instructed by the Grantor, Mints the Deed Token, with this Agreement attached to the Deed Metadata through the `operatingAgreement` field and the Deed Metadata identifying the matters stated in **Section 2.12**; a copy of the Certificate of Title or bill of sale may be attached to facilitate validation, but is not required for the Trust to be legally effective; and
   5. upon a Confirmed Transaction of the Mint, the Beneficiary is entitled to the Owner Rights.
2. The Trust is not intended to be, and shall not be treated as, a partnership, joint venture, corporation, or association. The Trust is revocable by the Grantor until the Mint: if title has been transferred into the Trust and the Mint does not occur, the Grantor may revoke the Trust, and the Trustee shall transfer title back to the Grantor. Upon the Mint, the Trust becomes irrevocable.
3. Where a Certificate of Title application is pending at the Mint, the Trust is nonetheless effective, and the Trustee shall complete the titling process at the Grantor's cost.

### 4. Transactions and Interactions with the Deed Contract

1. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, as among the Grantor, the Trustee, every Beneficiary and Beneficial Interest Holder, and every other person bound by or claiming under this Agreement or the Deed Token: (a) any signature or execution made through the use of private keys on a Blockchain Network for matters relating to the Deed Token or the Trust, made with intent to authenticate or adopt the operation or record to which it relates, is valid, sufficient, and final as if signed in writing; and (b) any other signed electronic record relating to the Deed Token or the Trust is valid and enforceable according to its terms. The parties intend each such signature and record to constitute an electronic signature and electronic record under the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001 et seq.) and applicable enactments of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act. The effect of any onchain act on a person not bound by this Agreement is determined by applicable law, as **Section 11** describes. Nothing in this Section purports to bind a stranger to this Agreement by contract alone.
2. Any action that the Beneficiary or the Trustee takes with respect to the Asset or the Trust is invalid unless first instructed through a Confirmed Transaction interacting with the Deed Contract, except for: (a) the Beneficiary's possession, use, operation, maintenance, insurance, registration, and leasing of the Asset, and collection of its income, to the extent the act does not purport to transfer or encumber the Deed Token, the Beneficial Interest, or title to the Asset, or to dissolve the Trust; (b) acts under **Sections 9.5 and 10.2 through 10.5**; (c) acts that this Agreement expressly permits by a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**, including those of the Trustee and the Trust Protector under **Section 13** and of a Program Sponsor under **Section 16**; (d) ministerial preservation acts; and (e) the Trustee's performance of its duties under **Section 8** pursuant to a direction or authorization given as this Agreement provides.
3. **Onchain Determinability; Good-Faith Reliance.** Beneficial ownership of the Trust, and the validity and effect of operations concerning the Deed Token, are determinable from the record of the Blockchain Network read together with this Agreement, subject only to mandatory law, to the Certificate of Title and other records that govern title to the Asset (**Section 11.6**), and to the offchain facts this Agreement makes relevant. A Transfer or other operation valid on that record is conclusive in favor of a person who in good faith and for value relies on it; a defect of assent, authority, or solicitation may be asserted between the parties to the operation, with remedies against the person who effected it, but does not unwind the position of such a person. A Transfer described in **Section 7.5.1** is governed by **Sections 7.5 and 11.4**.

### 6. Beneficiary Rights

1. **Owner Rights.** The Beneficiary, or an authorized third party, may execute any function through the Deed Contract concerning the Deed Token, the Asset, or the Trust — including transfer, encumbrance, fractionalization, or sale of the Deed Token, and transfer of the Deed Token into any Contract Account — and may dissolve the Trust under **Section 10**. The Beneficiary has the exclusive right to possess, use, operate, lease, and enjoy the Asset; is entitled to all net income and receipts from it (including rental income); may borrow against the Deed Token; and bears every cost, tax, fee, fine, and liability arising from the Asset or its use, as **Section 9** describes. An arrangement that would create an interest enforceable against title to the Asset — including a Lien of Record — requires direction to, and execution by, the Trustee. All of these rights are the "**Owner Rights**."
2. The Owner Rights of the present Beneficiary are absolute, sole, and uncontrolled during the period of its beneficial ownership, except as limited by this Agreement, by custody of the Deed Token in a Functional Contract (**Section 7.3**), or by the contingent interests of other Beneficial Interest Holders. The Beneficial Interest is freely alienable, subject only to any Transfer Policy applicable under **Section 16**, is not subject to any spendthrift restraint, and is subject to the claims of the Beneficiary's creditors to the extent applicable law provides.
3. **Fractional ownership.** If the Deed Token is fractionalized, the governance of the fractional interests — including use and possession of the Asset, decision thresholds, and deadlock — is governed by a separate agreement among the fractional owners. No platform, Trustee, or Trust Protector undertakes any managerial effort for fractional owners. Whether a fractional arrangement is a security depends on its substance and is not determined by this Agreement.

### 7. Deed Token Transfer

1. **Transfer of beneficial ownership.** The Beneficiary transfers the Owner Rights by Transferring the Deed Token. When the Deed Token is Transferred to an Account or Smart Wallet by, or with the authority of, the then-present Beneficiary (or as this Agreement otherwise gives effect, including under **Sections 7.6 and 7.7**), beneficial ownership vests in its controller. A valid transfer of beneficial ownership does not change the Trustee, and does not of itself change the Certificate of Title or require any filing with a Titling Authority. "**Ministerial preservation authority**" means, wherever used in this Agreement, authority to preserve the trust res and prevent waste, without power to make distributions or to direct or encumber the Asset. The Trustee's fiduciary duties run to all Beneficial Interest Holders, but as to future and contingent holders are limited to preserving the trust res and maintaining the integrity of the deed-asset relationship.
2. A valid transfer of beneficial ownership effects the full assignment of all interests, rights, duties, liabilities, and obligations of the Beneficiary under the Trust, except that liabilities arising from the Asset or its use before the transfer remain with the person who was the Beneficiary when they arose (**Section 9.3**).
3. **Custody is not ownership.** A Transfer of the Deed Token to a Smart Wallet transfers beneficial ownership to the persons who control it. A Transfer to a Functional Contract does not of itself transfer beneficial ownership; the depositing Beneficiary retains the Owner Rights until (a) the Deed Token is Transferred to an Account or Smart Wallet, (b) the Beneficiary explicitly assigns the Owner Rights by a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**, or (c) a Qualified Designation occurs. A "**Qualified Designation**" is a Confirmed Transaction executed by or through a Functional Contract that (i) consummates a sale or other disposition of the Beneficial Interest made by, or with the authority of, the then-present Beneficiary, (ii) designates an identified person (the "**Designated Owner**") as owner of record of the Beneficial Interest on the Functional Contract's records, and (iii) is supported by an onchain act or a signed record, made by or for the Designated Owner and satisfying **Section 4.1**, manifesting the Designated Owner's assent to take the Beneficial Interest on those terms. Upon a Qualified Designation, beneficial ownership passes to the Designated Owner notwithstanding that the Deed Token remains in the Functional Contract, the Functional Contract's custody is held for the Designated Owner, and its later release of the Deed Token confirms, and does not effect, that ownership. A designation without such assent is legally inert.
4. All identifying information about the chain of Addresses that have held the Deed Token is recorded on the Blockchain Network. An offchain database is not dispositive of beneficial ownership.
5. **Unauthorized Transfers.**
   1. A Transfer not made or authorized by the then-present Beneficiary and not otherwise given effect by this Agreement — including a Transfer resulting from theft or compromise of private keys or signing credentials, or from unauthorized use of an approval or delegation — is not a valid transfer of beneficial ownership, and the prior Beneficiary retains its position.
   2. The Beneficiary is responsible for the security of access to the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Deed Token.
   3. Nothing in this Section 7.5 limits the rights of a qualifying purchaser or protected secured party under **Section 11.4**.
   4. A conflict between a claim under this Section 7.5 and a claim under **Section 11.4** is resolved by a court of competent jurisdiction under **Section 12.4**.
6. **Succession on Death.** Upon the death of a Beneficiary who is a natural person, the Beneficial Interest passes as part of the deceased Beneficiary's personal property under the law governing the deceased Beneficiary's estate, and the Owner Rights are exercisable, without any court order as a condition of continuity, by the person who lawfully succeeds to control of the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Deed Token (the "**Key Successor**") — for the account of the person entitled under that law, where the two differ. A person who obtains access through theft or other unauthorized means is not a Key Successor and is governed by **Section 7.5**. Competing claims among successors are resolved by a court under **Section 12.4**, and do not unwind the position of a person who in good faith and for value relied on the acts of an apparent Key Successor. The death of a Beneficiary does not affect the office of Trustee. **Section 10.3** applies where no Key Successor exists.
7. **Unsolicited Transfers.** A transferee who did not solicit, initiate, or agree to acquire a Transfer may refuse the Beneficial Interest in whole — before any voluntary exercise of the Owner Rights, including taking possession or use of the Asset — by returning the Deed Token to the Address from which it was received or, where return is impossible, by Redeeming it or recording an unequivocal refusal through a Confirmed Transaction. A valid refusal relates back to the moment of the Transfer to the extent applicable law permits, and the transferor remains the Beneficiary throughout. Any exercise of the Owner Rights, other than an act reasonably necessary to preserve the Deed Token or the Asset, is acceptance. A Transfer that the transferee solicited, initiated, or agreed to acquire is accepted upon the Transfer itself. A disclaimant by Redeem leaves the transferor as continuing Beneficiary of a Trust whose Deed Token has been destroyed, who may proceed under **Section 10.2 or 10.4**.

### 8. Trustee Obligations and Safe Harbor

1. The Trustee shall (the "**Trustee Duties**"):
   1. act as the representative of the Trust solely upon the direction of the Beneficiary or the Trust Protector given as this Agreement provides (including the standing direction in **Section 9.5**), and hold title to the Asset consistently with this Agreement for the benefit of all Beneficial Interest Holders, without any power or duty to possess, operate, manage, lease, maintain, insure, or collect income from the Asset;
   2. hold and preserve title to the Asset in the Trust, join at the direction and expense of the Beneficiary in any defense of title that the Beneficiary conducts, and neither execute nor deliver any instrument transferring or encumbering title except as this Agreement directs and authorizes, maintaining the integrity of the corpus under **Section 1.5**;

### 10. Trust Termination

   1. the claimant delivers to the Trustee, and to the Trust Protector where the Trust Protector is not the claimant, a sworn affidavit, notarized, identifying the Asset, the Trust Name, the Deed ID, the Deed Contract, the Blockchain Network, and the last known holding Address, describing the circumstances of loss, stating that the Deed Token is to the claimant's knowledge held by an Account or Smart Wallet, and stating whether the claimant is in possession of the Asset and is shown as its registrant or operator (a "**Notice of Lost Token Control**");
   2. within ten (10) calendar days after that delivery, the claimant delivers a copy of the Notice of Lost Token Control by certified mail, return receipt requested, to every holder of a Lien of Record at the address stated in the Certificate of Title or financing statement, and to every secured party of record under a financing statement filed against the claimant as debtor covering the Deed Token or the Beneficial Interest, as disclosed by a search of the applicable UCC filing office of the claimant's location; and causes a Confirmed Transaction, directed to the holding Address or made through the notice function described in **Section 15.4**, identifying the Notice (the "**onchain notice leg**");
   3. a period of ninety (90) calendar days (the "**Quiet Period**") begins on the later of the Trustee's receipt of the Notice and the onchain notice leg. Any person with an interest in the Deed Token or the Asset may contest the claim during the Quiet Period by written notice to the Trustee and to the claimant, or by commencing legal proceedings concerning it and giving the Trustee written notice of them;
   4. a Confirmed Transaction originating from the holding Account or Smart Wallet during the Quiet Period halts the process; if the claimant recovered access, no further action is required, and otherwise the matter is resolved under **Section 10.4**;
   5. a contest during the Quiet Period halts the process, and the matter is resolved under **Section 10.4**;
   6. after the Quiet Period, the claimant delivers to the Trustee a sworn certificate stating that no transaction or contest described in clauses 4 and 5 occurred, that the deliveries described in clause 2 were made, and identifying the onchain notice leg; and
   7. **recovery safeguards:** the Trust Protector, unless it is the claimant, has standing to contest the claim; the Trustee shall not transfer title where it has actual knowledge of facts giving reasonable grounds to believe the claim is fraudulent or mistaken, but shall instead give notice of those facts to the claimant and the Trust Protector, whereupon the matter is resolved under **Section 10.4**; before transferring title, the Trustee shall itself review the publicly available record of the Blockchain Network to confirm the onchain notice leg and the absence of any transaction originating from the holding Address during the Quiet Period, and shall give the Trust Protector (unless it is the claimant) not less than ten (10) calendar days' notice of its intention to transfer; where the claimant is neither in possession of the Asset nor shown as its registrant or operator, the Trustee shall not transfer title except upon a court order under **Section 10.4**; and neither the Trustee nor the Trust Protector may shorten the Quiet Period, waive any requirement of this Section, or Transfer, freeze, or Redeem the Deed Token.

   Upon satisfaction of this Section 10.2, the Trustee transfers title to the Asset to the claimant, or to the person the claimant designates, subject to every Lien of Record; the substitute conditions of this Section replace the Redeem required by **Section 1.2(a)**; and the Deed Token becomes a Void Token.

### 11. Uniform Commercial Code Treatment

1. **The Deed Token.** The Deed Token is a "controllable electronic record" under Article 12 of the Uniform Commercial Code as adopted in California and, to the extent adopted, any other applicable jurisdiction ("**UCC Article 12**"), for so long as it is capable of being subjected to control. For purposes of UCC § 12-107(c)(1) (Cal. Com. Code § 12107(c)(1)), this Agreement — a record logically associated with the Deed Token and readily available for review — expressly provides that the State of California is the Deed Token's jurisdiction for purposes of UCC Article 12 and the Uniform Commercial Code. This designation concerns only matters governed by UCC Article 12 and the Uniform Commercial Code with respect to the Deed Token; it does not select the law governing the Trust (**Section 12.3**) or the Asset (**Section 11.6**).
2. **Two lanes of lending.** A financing may be secured in either or both of two lanes:
   1. **The token lane.** A security interest in the Deed Token (a controllable electronic record) and, where expressly granted, the Beneficial Interest (a general intangible), granted by the debtor in an authenticated security agreement or equivalent signed electronic record, attaches, is perfected, has priority, and is enforced under UCC Article 9 and UCC Article 12. Smart-contract standards for enforcement and disposition are agreed standards under UCC § 9-603 to the extent the debtor's security agreement identifies or incorporates them, and nothing in this Agreement waives any right that UCC § 9-602 makes nonwaivable, including the right of redemption under UCC § 9-623. A token-lane security interest is not a security interest in the Asset.
   2. **The asset lane.** A security interest in the Asset itself is created by the Trustee, as owner, at the Beneficiary's direction and as the Beneficiary's agent for the financing, and — for a Titled Asset — is perfected only by notation on the Certificate of Title or other compliance with the certificate-of-title statute, as UCC § 9-311(a)(2) provides; for Untitled Equipment, it is perfected as UCC Article 9 otherwise provides. The Trustee's grant of an asset-lane lien at the Beneficiary's direction secures only the obligations the Beneficiary directs, and imposes no personal liability on the Trustee.
   3. A lender that requires recourse to the physical Asset should take an asset-lane lien. Nothing in this Agreement varies any nonwaivable law that applies to a financing by reason of its substance, including consumer-protection, repossession, and notice rules applicable to financing secured by a consumer's vehicle; and nothing in this Agreement creates any right of self-help repossession, remote disablement, or similar remedy, which exists, if at all, only under the financing documents and applicable law.
3. **Custodial holding.** Where a person holds the Deed Token as custodian for another person under an agreement with that person, the custodian does not acquire the Beneficial Interest; it vests in the person for whom the Deed Token is ultimately held, as the custodian's records identify, and a transfer of that person's rights against the custodian operates as a transfer of the Beneficial Interest under **Section 7**.
4. **Qualifying purchasers and protected secured parties.** A person that obtains control of the Deed Token for value, in good faith, and without notice of a claim of a property right in the Deed Token or the Beneficial Interest (an "**Adverse Claim**") takes free of Adverse Claims as UCC § 12-104 provides. Every Beneficial Interest Holder agrees, as a term of the interest it acquires, that a person having control of the Deed Token has the power to transfer the Beneficial Interest to such a qualifying purchaser, and that its own claim is defeasible in favor of such a purchaser and of a secured party that obtains control on the same conditions, its remedy lying against the person who effected the unauthorized Transfer. A Lien of Record is not notice of an Adverse Claim; it binds the Asset under **Section 11.6** and is not a claim to the Deed Token.
5. The characterizations in this Section 11 have no bearing on whether the Deed Token is a security for any other purpose, and it is the intent of the parties that the Deed Token not be a security under federal or state securities laws.
6. **Asset-lane preservation.** Nothing in this Section 11 displaces the certificate-of-title statutes, titling and registration requirements, lien-notation rules, buyer-protection rules, entrustment rules, or other law governing the Asset. Control of the Deed Token determines rights in the Deed Token and the Beneficial Interest; it does not transfer, encumber, or create any interest in title to the Asset. A buyer of the Asset from the Trustee, a holder of a Lien of Record, and a protected purchaser of the Asset take according to that law. The law governing perfection and priority of a security interest in the Asset is the law that UCC Article 9 selects, including UCC §§ 9-301 and 9-303.
7. **Fallback without Article 12.** If no enactment of UCC Article 12 applies to a matter concerning the Deed Token, the assignment mechanics of this Agreement and applicable trust law continue to vest the Beneficial Interest in the person determined under **Section 7**, binding every person who claims under this Agreement or the Deed Token; only the third-party take-free protections vary, to the extent applicable law provides.

   2. **Powers.** The Trust Protector has only the powers: (a) to remove and replace the Trustee with any Qualified Trustee without amendment of this Agreement, to fill a vacancy in that office, and to designate or replace a Standby Trustee; (b) to correct any clerical error in the Deed Metadata, provided that a correction of the identification of the Asset may only conform it to the Certificate of Title or bill of sale by which the Asset was transferred into the Trust; (c) to consent to the election of an Agreement Version where **Section 16** so requires; (d) to receive notice of, and to contest, a claim under **Section 10.2 or 10.3**; (e) to designate its own successor; and (f) where **Section 16** applies, the further powers it expressly grants.
   3. **Limits.** The Trust Protector has no power to Transfer, encumber, freeze, or Redeem the Deed Token; to direct the sale, encumbrance, or other disposition of the Asset; to determine who is the Beneficiary; to alter the corpus; to amend this Agreement other than by a correction under **Section 13.4.2(b)**; to diminish the Owner Rights or the Protected Core; to shorten any Quiet Period or waive any requirement of **Section 10**; or to receive any income or proceeds of the Asset or any compensation. Any purported exercise of a power it does not have is void.
   4. **Standard.** A Trust Protector other than the Beneficiary exercises its powers in good faith, consistently with the Trust Purpose and the interests of the Beneficial Interest Holders, is a fiduciary only in that exercise, has no duty to monitor the Trustee or the Asset, and is liable only for its own willful misconduct or bad faith. To the extent the law of the State of Wyoming provides for trust protectors, trust directors, or directed trustees, the parties intend that the Trust Protector and the Trustee hold those offices with exactly the powers and limits stated in this Agreement.
5. **Validators generally.** A validator's review or validation of the Deed Token, the Deed Metadata, or the Asset is evidentiary only and confers no role in, power over, or interest in the Trust; and validation status does not affect beneficial ownership or the validity of the Trust.

### 15. Interoperability

1. Whether a Contract Account is a Smart Wallet or a Functional Contract is determined by its function, whoever operates it. Marketplace settlement contracts, lending pools, escrows, bridges, wrappers, and fractionalization contracts are Functional Contracts when they so function, and require no approval under this Agreement, although a Transfer Policy may limit at the level of the Deed Contract the contracts to which the Deed Token may be Transferred.
2. Where a wrapper or bridge holds the Deed Token and issues a representation of it, the wrapper or bridge is a Functional Contract, the holder of the representation is determined under **Sections 7.3 and 11.3**, this Agreement continues to govern, and any Transfer Policy applies to the representation.
3. The Beneficiary may move the Asset to another trust or arrangement represented by a token under another protocol by Redeeming the Deed Token and directing a transfer of title under **Section 10.1**, subject to Liens of Record and the rights of secured parties and Designated Owners; the Trustee shall cooperate to coordinate the Redeem, the transfer of title, and the issuance of the successor token.
4. **Notice and status functions.** Where the Deed Contract provides a function for posting notices or status records associated with the Deed Token, any notice this Agreement requires or permits to be given onchain may be given through it by a Confirmed Transaction, and the Trustee and the Trust Protector may use it for the notices this Agreement describes. A status record evidences, but does not determine, the status it records.

### 16. Program Terms

1. **Application.** This Section 16 applies to a Trust whose Deed Metadata, at its creation, identifies a Program, its Program Sponsor, and a program Transfer Policy — such as a fleet, rental, rent-to-own, equipment-sharing, or cooperative program — and continues until released under **Section 16.9**. Where it does not apply, it has no effect.
2. **Definitions and parameters.** The "**Program**" is the program identified in the Deed Metadata; the "**Program Sponsor**" is the person identified as its sponsor; a "**Participation Agreement**" is an agreement between a participant and the Program Sponsor under which the participant uses, rents, acquires, or finances an Asset; and a "**Member**" is a participant in good standing whose Account or Smart Wallet is registered as active in the registry the Deed Contract uses for the Program. The Deed Metadata states the following parameters; where one is not stated, the default applies: (a) **eligible transferees** — Members, the Program Sponsor and its designated affiliates, and Functional Contracts operated for the Program or by lenders the Program permits (default); (b) **right of first refusal** — whether **Section 16.4** applies and its period (default: does not apply; if applies, thirty (30) calendar days); (c) **acquisition mode** — completion transfer or financed designation under **Section 16.5** (default: completion transfer); (d) **arbitration** — whether **Section 16.8.3** applies and the arbitration organization (default: applies, with the American Arbitration Association under its Consumer Arbitration Rules); (e) **version consent** — whether the Trust Protector's consent is required for an Agreement Version election (default: required); and (f) **Standby Trustee** (default: none designated at creation).
3. **Trustee, Protector, and Transfer Policy.** The Program Sponsor is the Trust Protector (**Section 13**). While the Beneficiary is the Program Sponsor or an entity it wholly owns, directly or through other entities it wholly owns (a "**Sponsor Entity**"), that Sponsor Entity holds title to the Asset directly as Owner-Trustee under **Section 13.2.3** and is shown as owner on the Certificate of Title, unless the Deed Metadata or the Trust Protector designates a licensed or nonprofit trustee of a kind described in clause (a)(ii) or (a)(iii) of **Section 2.18**. The title administrator is never Trustee, and no Sponsor Entity is Trustee of a Trust whose Beneficiary is a different person. When a person that is not a Sponsor Entity becomes the Beneficiary, including by completion transfer or Qualified Designation under **Section 16.5**, that person becomes Owner-Trustee under **Section 13.2.3** (or, at its request, a licensed or nonprofit trustee the Trust Protector appoints serves instead), and the office of any Trustee that is a Sponsor Entity ends; the outgoing Trustee signs every document the Titling Authority requires to show the successor as owner. Neither the title administrator, the Program Sponsor, nor any person the Program Sponsor owns or controls may serve as Trustee of a Trust whose Beneficiary is not a Sponsor Entity, or hold itself out as available to do so. The Program Sponsor acts as Trust Protector in a capacity distinct from its capacity as counterparty under a Participation Agreement, lessor, lender, or holder of any right of first refusal, and shall not use its powers as Trust Protector to obtain an advantage in those capacities. Transfers of the Deed Token are permitted only to eligible transferees, subject to **Section 16.4**, except that a return under **Section 7.7**, a Redeem, a Transfer between Addresses of the same Beneficiary, a Transfer to or from a permitted Functional Contract, and succession under **Section 7.6** are always permitted. A successor who is not a Member holds the Beneficial Interest with all Owner Rights and may Transfer only to an eligible transferee.
4. **Right of first refusal.** Where it applies, before a Transfer to an eligible transferee other than the Program Sponsor, the Beneficiary gives the Program Sponsor notice of the proposed transferee, price, and terms, and the Program Sponsor may within the stated period elect to acquire the Deed Token on the same price and terms; failing election, the Beneficiary may complete the Transfer within ninety (90) calendar days on terms no more favorable to the transferee. A Transfer completed through the Deed Contract's program transfer mechanism is conclusively compliant as to every person other than the Beneficiary and the Program Sponsor.
5. **Acquisition and use.**
   1. **Completion transfer.** While a participant is renting, leasing, or acquiring the Asset under a Participation Agreement, the Program Sponsor (or the affiliate the Participation Agreement identifies) is the Beneficiary, and the participant's rights are contractual rights under the Participation Agreement — including any right of possession and use it grants — and not a Beneficial Interest. Upon completion of the participant's acquisition as the Participation Agreement defines it, the Beneficiary Transfers the Deed Token to the participant, who becomes the Beneficiary.
   2. **Financed designation.** The Deed Token may be held by a Functional Contract of the Program while the participant's financing is outstanding; the participant's signed Participation Agreement — identifying the Deed Token, the price and other material terms, and this Agreement, and stating that the participant's assent effects the acquisition of beneficial ownership of the Asset — is the assent required for a Qualified Designation under **Section 7.3**, and the participant becomes the Beneficiary from the Confirmed Transaction designating it. The financing is secured, if at all, as **Section 11.2** provides; any asset-lane lien must be noted on the Certificate of Title.
   3. In either mode, the Participation Agreement governs the participant's use, maintenance, insurance, and payment obligations, and **Section 9** applies to whoever is the Beneficiary. Every Participation Agreement is subject to the nonwaivable law that applies to it by reason of its substance, including rental, lease, rent-to-own, consumer-credit, and repossession law. Nothing in this Section 16 creates any right of self-help repossession, remote disablement, or location tracking, which exists, if at all, only under the Participation Agreement and applicable law.
   4. **Purpose and the Trustee's role.** Where a participant acquires the Asset under a Participation Agreement, a purpose of the Trust, in addition to the Trust Purpose, is to facilitate that acquisition as a title-transfer arrangement under which beneficial ownership of the Asset, and the participant's right to Redeem the Deed Token and receive title under **Section 10.1**, pass to the participant upon completion of the payments the Participation Agreement requires. The Trustee's role in that arrangement is ministerial — holding title and executing the instruments this Agreement directs — and it has no role in collecting payments, determining defaults, repossession, or enforcing the Participation Agreement.
   5. The Participation Agreement shall identify this Agreement, so that a participant's signed Participation Agreement is also a signed record of assent to this Agreement.
6. **No Trustee compensation; records agreement.** No compensation is payable to the Trustee by reason of its service as Trustee, except as **Section 13.3.3** permits for a licensed or nonprofit trustee that a Beneficiary has asked to serve; the Program Sponsor and every Sponsor Entity pay nothing to any Trustee. 8. **Disputes.**

9. **Release.** The Program Sponsor may release the application of this Section 16 by a Confirmed Transaction recording the release in the Deed Metadata; upon release, the Transfer Policy ends, the Trustee continues in office, and the Beneficiary becomes the Trust Protector unless it designates another person.
