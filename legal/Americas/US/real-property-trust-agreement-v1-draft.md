This Trust Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into by the Grantor through the creation of a Deed Token on the Deed Protocol. The identifying information of this trust (the "**Trust**") may be found in the Deed Token to which this Agreement is attached.

### How This Trust Works (Non-Operative Summary)

*This summary is provided for the reader's convenience only. It is not an operative part of this Agreement, creates no rights or obligations, and shall not be used to construe any operative provision. If this summary and any operative provision differ, the operative provision controls.*

- A trust holds recorded legal title to a specific piece of real property, exactly as real-property law provides. Where applicable law treats a trust as a relationship rather than an entity, that title is held by the trustee in a fiduciary capacity for the trust (or as local law otherwise requires).
- The entire present beneficial interest in that trust is personal property, and it belongs to whoever rightfully holds the Deed Token — or, while the token sits inside a smart-contract facility such as a loan pool, escrow, or bridge, to the person this Agreement's rules designate (usually the depositor, or a buyer who assented to a financed purchase). Transfer the token to a new rightful holder and the beneficial interest — with all its rights of possession, use, income, and disposition — passes to that holder automatically, under this Agreement.
- The token is not the land, and it is not the deed. It is the record that controls the trust. Record title to the land itself moves only by a recorded deed, and real-property law (liens, taxes, recording priority) always governs the land.
- The holder directs the trustee. The trustee is a title-holder only: it acts solely on instruction, never manages the property and never handles money. The trustee is either the owner itself (an **owner-trustee**, the usual case for a home owned by an individual) or an independent, qualified trustee such as a licensed trust company or a nonprofit trustee; only a licensed trust company or nonprofit trustee that the owner engages may be paid. Where the owner is its own trustee, the trust does not merge, because the future and contingent holders described in this Agreement always share the equitable interest, and a temporary continuity trustee springs into office if a court ever finds otherwise. A **trust protector** — the owner by default, or the sponsor of a program such as a housing cooperative — can replace the trustee. Neither the trustee nor the protector can move the token, change who owns it, or stop the owner from taking the land out of the trust.
- Some property belongs to a **program** — for example, a cooperative that keeps the land and leases it to the homeowner. Program terms built into this Agreement (Section 16) switch on from the token's onchain settings; they can limit who may buy and give the program a right of first refusal, but they cannot weaken the core protections that buyers, lenders, and heirs rely on (the "Protected Core").
- Wyoming law governs the trust. California's version of UCC Article 12 governs the token as a digital record (who controls it, and when a good-faith buyer or lender takes it free of other claims). The land itself is always governed by the law of the place where it sits. This Agreement covers real property only.
- If someone sends the token to your wallet without your agreement, you presumptively own the beneficial interest from the moment of receipt — including for taxes and the other burdens of ownership — and can use every ownership right immediately. You can refuse the interest entirely by an onchain act (ordinarily by sending the token back, or by burning it); a valid refusal erases that ownership retroactively to the day you received it, to the extent the law allows. Some laws — including federal tax law — honor the refusal fully only if you act promptly after learning of the transfer, so refuse quickly. But moving, selling, listing, or using the token in any way other than sending it back or burning it counts as permanently accepting the property — backdated to the day you received it (exceptions: a listing you cancel, or that expires, without any sale does not count; nor do acts needed merely to preserve the token or the property while you decide; and where sending it back and burning it are both impossible, you can still refuse by recording your refusal onchain — after which you can hold the token only as a custodian for its rightful owner).
- A thief gains nothing by stealing the token. But if a thief sells it to an innocent buyer who pays value and takes control without notice of the theft, that buyer may keep the token and the beneficial interest, and your remedy is against the thief. Secure your keys, and act immediately on any theft.
- If the land is lost by operation of law (tax sale, foreclosure of a recorded lien, condemnation), the trust dissolves, the token becomes void, and the token holder keeps the wind-up rights to any proceeds. If the holder dies, whoever lawfully succeeds to control of the wallet keeps operating immediately, without waiting for a court — acting for whoever inherits under estate law; merely finding someone's keys does not make access lawful. The beneficial interest passes as personal property under the law governing the holder's estate. If token keys are lost, or no one lawfully succeeds to key access, this Agreement provides recorded, contestable procedures to unwind the trust; the trustee and the protector are notified of every such claim, the protector can contest it, and the trustee will not sign on a claim it has reason to believe is false — but neither can shorten the process or touch the token. Burning the token is permanent and can never be undone: done by the owner, it is the first step of taking the land out of the trust by deed (done by a recipient who never accepted, it is a refusal, as described above).
- Lending against the token is personal-property secured lending under UCC Article 9 against the token and, where expressly granted, the beneficial interest; it is never structured as a mortgage of the land itself. The one exception is a home loan to an owner who lives in the home: that is a residential mortgage loan, secured by a recorded deed of trust that the trustee signs at the owner's direction, with any pledge of the token only alongside it.

### Recitals

- Whereas blockchain Smart Contracts provide a secure and interoperable way of representing ownership of digital assets through the use of an electronic ledger to immutably track, document, and verify all transactions. As a consequence, new systems and methods are emerging to store, interact with, and transact on real and digital assets.
- Whereas the grantor wants to link ownership of real property to a Deed Token so as to benefit from digital operations and services.
- Whereas on issuance of the Deed Token, the grantor will attach this Agreement to the Deed Token, and intends for this Agreement to act as the trust agreement governing the trust that holds the Property. For the purposes of interoperability and standardization, the grantor and any subsequent party to this agreement intend to keep all the identifying information of this Agreement stored in the Deed Token to which this contract has been attached. All the operations, definitions, and procedures related to the Deed Token and the Trust will be stored in this Agreement.
- Whereas the purpose and design of this Trust allow the beneficiary to use blockchain technology to facilitate transfer and utilization of property ownership. To the extent provided in this Agreement, the trustee's power and authority to deal with the Property are subject to the direction of the beneficiary, who holds all present economic and possessory rights to the Property, subject to the contingent equitable interests of determinable future beneficiaries and legal successors. The beneficiary (who may change from time to time and is at all times the person determined under **Section 7** of this Agreement, which may differ from the person having custody or control of the Deed Token) is entitled to income, maintains the sole right of termination, and is intended to be treated as the owner of the Property for federal, state, and local tax purposes to the maximum extent applicable tax law permits.
- Whereas the parties intend, in the tradition of the American land trust, that the entire beneficial interest in the Trust is personal property — an interest in the Trust, and not an estate or interest in land — freely assignable by the digital mechanics of this Agreement, while title to the Property itself remains held, transferred, and encumbered only under the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located.
- Whereas the parties intend that beneficial ownership of the Trust, and the validity and effect of operations concerning the Deed Token, be determinable from the record of the Blockchain Network read together with this Agreement, subject only to mandatory law (including sanctions law and judicial process) and to instruments recorded in the real property records as this Agreement provides, and they adopt **Section 4.3** to that end.
- Whereas the parties intend to treat all valid operations done via blockchain on the Deed Token as final as among all persons bound by this Agreement, with the same force and effect as a fully executed agreement; the effect of such operations on persons not bound by this Agreement is determined by applicable law, including Article 12 of the Uniform Commercial Code where enacted, as described in **Section 11**. Further, the parties intend to treat digital signatures with the same legal consequence as wet signatures.
- Now, therefore, in consideration of the agreements and obligations set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

### 1. Trust Purpose ###

1. The purpose of the Trust is to create a legally enforceable link such that ownership of the Deed Token determines ownership of the Property, so as to allow for time-efficient, cost-effective, accurate, and secure ownership, transfer, use, and enjoyment of real property title in the digital environment (the "**Trust Purpose**").
2. On creation, the Trust will take and hold title to the Property, and ownership of the Trust will be represented by ownership of the Deed Token. Regardless of the sale, hypothecation, or other transfer of the Deed Token, title to the Property will remain in the Trust until such time as either the then-present Beneficiary (or another authorized party) distributes the Property out of the Trust, or record title is transferred out of the Trust by operation of law as described in **Section 10.5**. To facilitate the Trust Purpose and maintain the integrity of the deed-property relationship, the Trustee shall not execute or deliver any deed or other agreement transferring the Property out of the Trust unless and until (a) the Deed Token has been Redeemed (**Section 2.14**), making it permanently untransferable, or (b) the dissolution is authorized under **Sections 10.2, 10.3, or 10.4** of this Agreement or required under the perpetuities savings provision of **Section 12.5**.
3. Subject to **Sections 6 and 7**, the owner(s) of the Account or Smart Wallet holding the Deed Token shall be the Beneficiary of the Trust (the office of Trustee being governed by **Section 13**). All rights and obligations in this Agreement shall at all times be linked to the owner of the Deed Token, provided that such ownership is established through a valid Account or Smart Wallet, and not merely through temporary custody by a Functional Contract. Rightful ownership (as distinct from temporary custody) of the Deed Token confers full present beneficial ownership of the Property, including all economic and possessory rights, subject to the contingent equitable interests of other Beneficial Interest Holders as defined in **Section 2.26**.
4. **Nature of the Beneficial Interest.** The entire beneficial interest in the Trust — comprising the Owner Rights of the present Beneficiary and every contingent interest of the other Beneficial Interest Holders (collectively, the "**Beneficial Interest**") — is, and is hereby declared by the Grantor and agreed by every person who at any time claims any interest under or through this Agreement or the Deed Token to be, **personal property**. No Beneficiary or other Beneficial Interest Holder holds, by virtue of the Beneficial Interest, any legal or equitable estate in the Property as land; the Beneficiary's rights in respect of the Property — including the rights described in **Section 6.1** — are held under, and exercised through, the Trust and this Agreement, and are in no way diminished by this characterization. This characterization is a term of the Trust fixed at its creation, in the tradition of the American land trust, and controls whenever the characterization of the Beneficial Interest is at issue. References in this Agreement to a transfer, assignment, disclaimer, holding, encumbrance, or vesting of the Beneficial Interest mean the present beneficial interest of the Beneficiary — exclusive of the contingent interests of the other Beneficial Interest Holders, which are neither transferred nor extinguished by any such event and continue in the class as **Sections 2.26 and 12.5** provide — except where this Agreement expressly refers to the interests of the Beneficial Interest Holders collectively. Accordingly:
   - (a) the Beneficial Interest passes by assignment under **Section 7** upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership, without a deed; no transfer of the Deed Token, and no assignment of the Beneficial Interest, is a conveyance of an interest in land; and, to the extent any applicable law requires an assignment of the Beneficial Interest to be made by a signed writing, the electronic records and signatures described in **Section 4.1**, together with this Agreement, satisfy that requirement;
   - (b) a security interest in the Deed Token or in the Beneficial Interest — in each case to the extent expressly granted by the debtor in an authenticated security agreement or equivalent signed electronic record satisfying UCC § 9-203 (see **Section 11.2**) — attaches, is perfected, has priority, and is enforced under Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code as personal-property collateral, and not under the law governing mortgages of, or foreclosure upon, real property (see **Sections 11.2 and 11.7**); and
   - (c) succession to the Beneficial Interest on the death of a Beneficiary is governed by the law governing intangible personal property and by **Section 7.6** of this Agreement.

   The operative mechanism that transfers the Beneficial Interest is the assignment effected by this Agreement itself upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership under **Section 7**, of which the Deed Token is the authoritative record and instrument of transfer; **Section 11.7** states the relationship between that assignment mechanism and the treatment of the Deed Token as a controllable electronic record under **Section 11**. Nothing in this **Section 1.4** limits **Section 11.6**: the Property itself is and remains real property, title to which is held, transferred, and encumbered only in accordance with the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located.
5. **Integrity of the Trust Corpus.** It is a principle of this Agreement that the corpus of the Trust is fixed at the creation of the Trust: the corpus consists of the Property as described at that time (**Section 2.12**), together with the legal appurtenances and accessions of, and the proceeds arising from, that Property under applicable law and this Agreement, and of nothing else. For as long as the Property is operated through the Deed Token, no direction of any Beneficiary, no act of the Trustee, and no instrument of any third party may substitute any other asset for the corpus, add any asset to it, or convey any part of it out of the Trust (**Section 1.2**); the Trustee shall refuse, and has no power to accept, any purported standalone addition to the Trust — including any deed or other instrument by which any person, including a stranger to this Agreement, purports to convey any other property, and whatever liabilities attach to it, into the Trust — and any such purported addition is void as to the Trust, vests nothing in it, and imposes no duty, debt, tax attribute, or liability on the Trust, the Trustee, or any Beneficial Interest Holder (**Section 8.1.2**). The only exceptions are those effected by mandatory law and the events described in **Section 10**, each of which leaves a recorded trace. Dealings that this Agreement authorizes — including leases and encumbrances of, and other dealings with, the Property under **Sections 6.1 and 8.1**, the collection and distribution of income and proceeds to the Beneficiary under **Sections 6.1 and 10.7**, and every transfer, pledge, custody, or other operation concerning the Deed Token or the Beneficial Interest under **Sections 7 and 11** — are exercises of rights in or through the corpus, not alterations of its composition; and the way interests in the Property arise and bind the Trust's title under the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located (**Sections 11.6 and 11.8**) is unaffected by this Section. Any ambiguity concerning the composition of the corpus, or concerning the effect of any act or instrument upon it, shall be resolved in favor of the principle stated in this Section. Upon dissolution of the Trust, this principle is released in full: nothing in this Section limits the freedom of the Beneficiary, or of any other person entitled under **Section 10** to direct conveyance, to direct the deed of distribution in any lawful manner, including a conveyance to one or more grantees and a division of the Property, or of distinct rights in it, among them.

### 2. Definitions ###

1. **Account** means an Externally Owned Account (EOA) on a Blockchain Network, which is an Address controlled directly by one or more private keys held by individuals or entities.
2. **Address** means a public key address on a Blockchain Network.
3. **Beneficiary** means the individual(s) or entity(ies) holding present beneficial ownership of the Trust and the Owner Rights as determined under **Section 7**. Beneficial ownership is ordinarily established by control of the Account or Contract Account holding the Deed Token, but may differ from the current onchain holder due to Functional Contract custody (**Section 7.3(b)**), a Qualified Designation during such custody (**Section 7.3(c)**), custodial holding (**Section 11.3**), succession on death (**Section 7.6**), disclaimer of an unsolicited Transfer (**Section 7.7**), unauthorized transfers (**Section 7.5**), or alternative dissolution upon loss of token control (**Sections 10.2 through 10.5**). The Beneficiary is one of the Beneficial Interest Holders, but the term "Beneficiary" as used in this Agreement refers to the present interest holder unless otherwise indicated. In the event the Deed Token has not yet been minted, or has been minted but has not yet been owned by any Account, the Beneficiary shall be the original Grantor. In the event ownership of the Deed Token is fractionalized (as discussed further in **Sections 6.1, 6.3, and 7.3**), then the defined term Beneficiary as used in this Agreement shall apply to the various owners of the Deed Token as a group.
4. **Blockchain Network** means any distributed ledger technology network used to maintain records of ownership and management of the Deed Token to which this Agreement is attached. The relevant blockchain network may change due to actions of the Beneficiary, including the wrapping of the Deed Token to a different blockchain network or the minting of the Deed Token on a different blockchain network.
5. **Confirmed Transaction** means a transaction that has been recorded on the Blockchain Network and has become final under the consensus and settlement rules of that Blockchain Network. A transaction on Ethereum is final when that transaction becomes part of a block on the chain with a supermajority link between two checkpoint blocks. Where the Blockchain Network is a rollup or other network that publishes its transaction data to a Settlement Layer for finality, a transaction is a Confirmed Transaction only when the transaction data containing it has been included in a block of the Settlement Layer that is itself final under the Settlement Layer's rules. A transaction that has only been accepted, ordered, or reported by a sequencer, or included in a block that is not yet final, is not a Confirmed Transaction. Other Blockchain Networks maintain similar or different definitions of finality, which apply in the event of the transfer or minting of the Deed Token on such a Blockchain Network.
6. **Contract Account** means an Address controlled by a Smart Contract. A Contract Account may either: (a) serve as a method of holding Deed Tokens under the direct control of specific individuals or entities who thereby become Beneficiaries (a "**Smart Wallet**"), or (b) operate as a functional contract (such as escrow, bridge, or collateral management) where custody does not automatically confer beneficial ownership (a "**Functional Contract**").
7. **Creation Date** means the date and time at which the Trust was created by the Grantor.
8. **Ethereum** means the Ethereum mainnet and the consensus blockchain for such mainnet (networkID:1, chainID:1) as recognized by the official Go Ethereum Client implemented at https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum as of the Creation Date.
9. **Grantor** is the individual or entity who creates the Trust and grants the Property to the Trust.
10. **Mint** means a Confirmed Transaction executing the issuance function of the Deed Contract (in the Deed Contract deployed as of the Creation Date, `mintAsset`) that results in the association of a Deed ID to an Address.
11. **Token** means a digital asset tracked by the Deed Contract on the Blockchain Network under a unique identification code (Deed ID), implemented under the ERC-721 non-fungible token standard or under any other token standard used by the Deed Contract, or by a successor Deed Contract under **Section 14.3.4**, for the Deed Token. In the event the Deed Token to which this Agreement is attached references an alternative Blockchain Network, such Deed Token may follow a different Token standard, or utilize a different definition of Token.
12. **Property** is the bundle of rights identified in the legal description stored in the Deed Metadata under the fields `definition.claim` and `definition.offchainRegistrar`. The Property so described is the bundle of rights which is to be deeded into the Trust and held throughout the life of the Trust. This Agreement applies only where the Property is real property, including land, improvements, and leasehold and other estates in real property; a Token representing any other kind of asset is not a Deed Token for purposes of this Agreement and is not governed by it.
13. **Deed Token** means a Token issued using the Deed Contract. In this case, such Deed Token should be assumed to reference the token and standard on the Blockchain Network on which that Deed Token is tracked. In the event a Deed Token is wrapped, or transferred, to an alternative Blockchain Network, the Deed Token will reference the ultimate controlling interest Token at any present moment.
14. **Redeem** means the result of a Confirmed Transaction executing the retirement function of the Deed Contract (in the Deed Contract deployed as of the Creation Date, `burnAsset` or `burnBatchAssets`) that results in the permanent locking or removal of the Deed Token from circulation. In the event the Deed Token has been wrapped, or transferred, to an alternative Blockchain Network, it may be required to unwrap, or transfer back, the Deed Token to the original Blockchain Network to execute the retirement function. Depending on which Blockchain Network the Deed Token was minted, the specific function names may be different, but Redeeming such token shall refer to effectively locking the Deed Token so that it becomes untransferable forever into the future.
15. **Smart Contract** means the bytecode deployed on a specific Blockchain Network Address which acts as a program to execute and run a series of processes or interactions.
16. **Deed Contract** means the Smart Contract used to maintain records of ownership and management of the Deed Token to which this Agreement is attached.
17. **Deed ID** means the unique and immutable identifier determined on Trust creation and assigned to the corresponding Deed Token. The Deed ID is generated from fields of the Deed Metadata that include a cryptographic hash of the precise language of this Agreement and of the parameters stated in the Deed Metadata at the creation of the Trust, so that the Deed ID itself commits to the terms governing the Trust. A Deed ID that has been assigned to a Deed Token shall never be assigned to any other Token, including after the Deed Token has been Redeemed or has become a Void Token; any Token later bearing the same identifier is not the Deed Token and confers no interest under this Agreement.
18. **Deed Metadata** means the data directly stored within the Deed Token as well as external data stored on IPFS (or similar immutable storage system) and linked within the Deed Token itself (using the fields `definition` and `configuration`). These will always include Deed ID, Trust Name (stored as `definition.holdingEntity`), and Property legal description (`definition.claim`). Other additional information may be included to simplify property identification, verify past ownership and other activities. Alternative Blockchain Networks may utilize different data configurations or definitions. The Deed Metadata shall also identify — in the `configuration` field or in onchain traits of the Deed Token that the Deed Contract reserves against change except as this Agreement provides — the Trustee, any Standby Trustee, the Trust Protector (or that the Beneficiary is the Trust Protector), the content hash of this Agreement, and every parameter stated under **Section 14.1**, including, where **Section 16** applies, the Program, the Program Sponsor, the Title Administrator, the Ground Lease, and the Program parameters.
19. **Transfer** means any operation performed through the Deed Contract that assigns the Deed Token to a new non-null Address.
20. **Trustee** means the Qualified Trustee serving as trustee of the Trust under **Section 13**, or its successor.
21. **Trust Name** means the name of the Trust, more particularly defined in the Deed Metadata (in `definition.holdingEntity`). It is recommended to encode the Deed ID in the Trust Name using Base32 Crockford encoding.
22. **Alternative Dissolution Event** means a dissolution of the Trust under **Sections 10.2, 10.3, 10.4, or 10.5**, or upon the Perpetuities Termination Date under **Section 12.5**, without prior Redemption of the Deed Token.
23. **Notice of Lost Token Control** means a sworn affidavit (or other sworn recordable instrument permitted by applicable law), in recordable form, notarized in the form (acknowledgment, jurat, or equivalent) required by the recording statutes of the jurisdiction where the Property is located, recorded with the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority in that jurisdiction, declaring that the Beneficiary (or their legal successor) has lost the ability to Redeem the Deed Token (or, in the case described in **Section 10.2.7**, that the Deed Token has been Redeemed by another by way of disclaimer, rendering Redemption by the Beneficiary impossible). The Notice shall identify: the full legal description of the Property (matching the legal description stored in the Deed Metadata under `definition.claim`) and the assessor parcel number where the county uses one; the Trust Name exactly as the Property is vested of record (and the name of the record titleholder, if stated differently of record); the recording reference (instrument number or book and page) of the deed by which the Property was conveyed into the Trust; the name of the claimant; a mailing address at which the claimant, and the Trustee if different, will receive contest notices and deliveries under **Section 10.2.2**; the Deed ID, the Deed Contract address, the Blockchain Network, and the last known Account or Contract Account holding the Deed Token. The Notice shall describe the circumstances of loss, shall state that, to the best of the claimant's knowledge, the Deed Token is held by an Account or Smart Wallet at the time of recording (or the disclaimer-Redemption statement described in **Section 10.2.7**), and shall request indexing against both the record titleholder (the Trust) and the claimant.
24. **Quiet Period** means a period of ninety (90) calendar days commencing on the date a Notice of Lost Token Control is recorded with the relevant county recorder or equivalent recording authority, during which any interested party may contest the Notice or assert a competing claim to the Property or the Deed Token.
25. **Void Token** means a Deed Token that has been dissociated from the Property and the Trust following an Alternative Dissolution Event. A Void Token confers no beneficial ownership rights, no Owner Rights, and no interest in the Property, regardless of its continued existence on the Blockchain Network. For the avoidance of doubt, while a Void Token may continue to exist as a controllable electronic record under UCC Article 12, it shall confer no rights under this Agreement except as expressly provided in **Sections 10.5.2 and 10.5.4**.
26. **Beneficial Interest Holders** means, collectively, (a) the present Beneficiary, (b) any person who would become a Beneficiary upon a valid Transfer of the Deed Token under the objective mechanics of **Section 7** (including transfer to an Account under Section 7.1, transfer to a Smart Wallet under Section 7.3(a), explicit assignment of Owner Rights under Section 7.3(b)(ii), or a Qualified Designation under Section 7.3(c)), and (c) any successor who would acquire a beneficial interest through the succession provisions of **Sections 7.6 and 10.3**, including any Key Successor and the persons who would take under the law governing a deceased Beneficiary's estate. The interests described in clauses (b) and (c) are genuine, defeasible contingent equitable interests in the Trust, arising simultaneously with the creation of the Trust and determined by the objective transfer and succession mechanics of this Agreement rather than by the identity of any specific future holder. The inclusion of future and contingent interests in this definition does not confer any present right of action, possession, or control upon any person other than the present Beneficiary, except for the narrow procedural standing to petition for appointment of a Continuity Trustee under **Section 12.6** following a suspension of the Trustee's office or a judicial determination of merger and the failure to designate a Continuity Trustee within the time period specified therein (standing that may be exercised only by a person claiming a present interest or an interest by succession or designation, not by a stranger asserting only the possibility of a future transfer). This definition does not alter the tax treatment of the Trust under **Section 12.7**.
27. **Beneficial Interest** has the meaning given in **Section 1.4**.
28. **Key Successor** means the person who, after a Beneficiary's death, lawfully succeeds to control of the Account or Smart Wallet holding or controlling the Deed Token — whether under the deceased Beneficiary's estate plan or lifetime arrangements, under applicable succession law, or under the recovery or succession features of a Smart Wallet. A person who obtains access through theft, compromise, misappropriation, or other unauthorized means is not a Key Successor and is governed by **Section 7.5**.
29. **Trust Protector** means the person determined under **Section 13.4.1**, holding only the powers stated in **Section 13.4**.
30. **Qualified Validator** means a person that is registered and active as a validator in the Validator Registry.
31. **Validator Registry** means the onchain registry of validators maintained as part of the Deed Protocol and identified by the Deed Contract.
32. **Deed Protocol** means the open-source system of Smart Contracts — including the Deed Contract and the Validator Registry — together with the agreements published for use with it, through which Deed Tokens are issued and maintained.
33. **Agreement Version** means a version of this Agreement adopted and published with its content hash as described in **Section 14.2**.
34. **Transfer Policy** means a restriction on the persons to whom, or the manner in which, the Deed Token may be Transferred, applicable under **Section 16** and implemented by the Deed Contract.
35. **Protected Core** means the provisions described in **Section 14.1.2**.
36. **Settlement Layer** means Ethereum, or any other Blockchain Network to which the Blockchain Network on which the Deed Token is recorded publishes its transaction data for finality.

### 7. Deed Token Transfer

1. The Beneficiary may transfer the Owner Rights by transferring the Deed Token to another Account or Contract Account. When the Deed Token is transferred to an Account (EOA), beneficial ownership vests in the owner of that Account upon the Transfer where the Transfer is made or authorized by the then-present Beneficiary or is otherwise given effect by this Agreement (including under **Sections 7.6 and 7.7**); a release or distribution of the Deed Token by a Functional Contract vests beneficial ownership in accordance with the entitlement determined under this Agreement, and not by reason of raw receipt alone. When transferring to a Contract Account, the determination of beneficial ownership depends on the nature of the Contract Account as defined in **Section 7.3**. A valid transfer of beneficial ownership does not change the Trustee: the office of Trustee is governed by **Section 13**, and the Trustee takes direction from the person who is the Beneficiary under this Section 7 at the time the direction is given. "**Ministerial preservation authority**" means, for every use of that phrase in this Agreement, authority to preserve the trust res and prevent waste, without power to make distributions or to direct or encumber the Property, on the pattern stated in **Section 12.6**. The Trustee's fiduciary duties under this Agreement run to all Beneficial Interest Holders; provided, however, that as to future and contingent Beneficial Interest Holders, such duties are limited to preserving the trust res and maintaining the integrity of the deed-property relationship, and do not create any present right of action, direction, or control in any person other than the present Beneficiary (except for the narrow procedural standing described in **Sections 2.26 and 12.6**).
2. Transfer of the beneficial ownership of the Trust via valid transfer of the Deed Token, or via a Qualified Designation under **Section 7.3(c)**, shall effectuate the full assignment of all interests, rights, duties, liabilities, and obligations under the Trust, subject, in the case of an unsolicited Transfer, to the transferee's right of disclaimer under **Section 7.7**. Therefore, use of the terms "Trustee" and "Beneficiary" within this Agreement shall reference the present Trustee and present Beneficiary, as determined by the rules set forth in this Section 7.
3. The Beneficiary may transfer custody of the Deed Token to a Contract Account, which may operate in one of two ways:

   a) As a **Smart Wallet**: a multi-signature wallet, account-abstraction wallet, or other custody solution directly controlled by specific individuals or entities. The transfer to a Smart Wallet operates as a transfer of beneficial ownership to the individual(s) or entity(ies) who control it, and they become the new Beneficiary(ies).

   b) As a **Functional Contract**: an escrow, bridge, lending protocol, collateral management system, fractionalization mechanism, or similar utility. The transfer of the Deed Token to a Functional Contract does not automatically constitute a transfer of beneficial ownership. The original Beneficiary retains their Owner Rights and position as Beneficiary until either: (i) the Deed Token is transferred to an Account or Smart Wallet, (ii) the Beneficiary explicitly assigns their Owner Rights through a separate legal instrument, which may be a signed electronic record satisfying **Section 4.1**, or (iii) beneficial ownership passes under a Qualified Designation as provided in **Section 7.3(c)**.

   c) **Qualified Designation: transfer of beneficial ownership during Functional Contract custody.** Beneficial ownership may change hands while the Deed Token remains in the custody of one or more Functional Contracts, but only as provided in this Section 7.3(c).

      1. A "**Qualified Designation**" means a Confirmed Transaction executed by or through one or more Functional Contracts that satisfies all of the following: (A) it consummates a sale, exchange, or other disposition of the Beneficial Interest made by, or with the authority of, the then-present Beneficiary — including by satisfying the terms on which that Beneficiary listed, offered, or committed the Deed Token for sale or disposition; (B) it designates an identified person (the "**Designated Owner**") as the owner of record of the Beneficial Interest on the records of a Functional Contract — for example, as the borrower-of-record of a lending protocol whose own records entitle that person to redemption of the collateral upon repayment, to any liquidation surplus, and to control of refinancing; and (C) it is supported by Qualifying Assent Evidence of the Designated Owner as defined below. A Qualified Designation may be executed by any party — including a payment processor, relayer, or other intermediary submitting the transaction on the parties' behalf — and the identity of the transaction's sender does not determine beneficial ownership; the designation and the Qualifying Assent Evidence do.
      2. "**Qualifying Assent Evidence**" means an onchain act, or a signed electronic record, manifesting the Designated Owner's assent to take the Beneficial Interest on the terms of the designating operation. Qualifying Assent Evidence exists in each of the following cases:
         - **(A) Initiation.** The Confirmed Transaction effecting the designation, or the operation it executes, was signed or initiated by, or upon the signed instruction of, the Designated Owner's Account or Smart Wallet, where that signature or instruction binds the terms of the operation and is evidenced on the Blockchain Network.
         - **(B) Signed record of assent.** A signed electronic record manifesting that assent, made onchain or offchain, and valid under **Section 4.1** and the statutes described in it. Any record manifesting that assent qualifies, whatever its form, wording, or medium, and by whomever captured: this Agreement prescribes no form of words, no disclosure text, and no interface, and a record made offchain in the ordinary course of the transaction (such as a purchase or checkout confirmation) qualifies without being recorded on, carried in, or referenced by any operation on the Blockchain Network. **Safe harbor.** A record under this clause (B) that (i) identifies the Deed Token; (ii) identifies or objectively bounds the operation and its material economic terms; (iii) identifies or incorporates by reference this Agreement (a reference or description sufficient to locate it suffices); and (iv) states in substance that the Designated Owner's assent effects the acquisition of beneficial ownership of real property, with the incidents of that ownership, is conclusively sufficient as to its adequacy as a manifestation of assent; the absence of any of these elements does not disqualify a record that otherwise manifests assent. **Anchored records.** Where the designating operation includes a cryptographic commitment to the record, the record is presumed authentic and presumed to have been assented to as of that operation; the presumption is rebuttable as between the parties to the designation, but it survives the unavailability of any platform, service, or person that captured or stored the record, the commitment itself evidencing the record's existence and timing. A record not so anchored remains fully valid. The burden of producing the record lies on the party who made, or who relies upon, the designation; if the record is not produced when the designation is questioned, the designation shall be treated as unassented as between the parties to it (legally inert under clause 5 of this Section 7.3(c), curable only under clause 2(C)), subject in every case to **Section 4.3.2** as to persons who in good faith relied on the designation. Nothing in this Agreement imposes on the Trustee, or on any platform or service provider, any duty to obtain, retain, produce, or verify any such record, and no platform or service provider is a party to a designation merely because it facilitated, transmitted, verified, or stored an operation or record.
         - **(C) Acceptance by conduct.** The Designated Owner's subsequent exercise, by a Confirmed Transaction signed or initiated by the Designated Owner's Account or Smart Wallet, of any right that the Functional Contract's records reserve to the owner of record — including repayment, refinancing, or redemption of collateral. Passive receipt or retention of a payment or distribution initiated by another party is not acceptance by conduct.

         This clause is defined by function, not by technology: any act or record satisfying its terms qualifies, under whatever technical standard it is implemented, without amendment of this Agreement.
      3. **Effect.** Upon a Qualified Designation supported by Qualifying Assent Evidence described in clause 2(A) or 2(B), beneficial ownership of the Trust and the Owner Rights pass to the Designated Owner at the Confirmed Transaction effecting the designation, notwithstanding that the Deed Token remains in the custody of a Functional Contract. Where the Qualifying Assent Evidence is acceptance by conduct under clause 2(C), beneficial ownership and the Owner Rights pass to the Designated Owner upon the Confirmed Transaction constituting that acceptance, prospectively and without relation back. From the moment beneficial ownership passes: the Functional Contract's custody of the Deed Token is held for the Designated Owner, and the Functional Contract's records stating that entitlement constitute an acknowledgment that control of the Deed Token is held on behalf of the Designated Owner within the meaning of UCC § 12-105(e), so that the Designated Owner has control of the Deed Token for purposes of UCC Article 12 and **Section 11.4** from that moment, without the acknowledgment imposing any duty on the Functional Contract or any other person (UCC § 12-105(g)); the Designated Owner is the Beneficiary for all purposes of this Agreement, including **Sections 6.1 and 12.7**; and the transferor's beneficial ownership and Owner Rights terminate simultaneously, so that at every moment exactly one present Beneficiary (or one group of fractional owners treated as the Beneficiary under **Sections 2.3 and 6.3**) holds the present Beneficial Interest. Upon satisfaction of the secured obligation or other termination of the Functional Contract's custody, the Functional Contract's release of the Deed Token to the Designated Owner's Account or Smart Wallet confirms — and does not itself effect — the beneficial ownership already vested under this Section. A Qualified Designation is not a fractionalization of the Deed Token.
      4. **Assignment instrument.** The Qualifying Assent Evidence, together with the Confirmed Transaction effecting the designation, constitutes the explicit assignment of Owner Rights described in **Section 7.3(b)(ii)**, and is a signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**. No separate offchain instrument is required.
      5. **No unilateral imposition.** A designation without Qualifying Assent Evidence is legally inert: the naming of any person as borrower-of-record or owner of record by a Functional Contract, by the sender of a transaction, or by any other party does not — by itself — transfer beneficial ownership to that person, impose on that person any duty, debt, tax attribute, or liability under **Section 6.1 or Section 12.7**, or alter the rule of **Section 7.3(b)** that custody does not confer beneficial ownership. Until Qualifying Assent Evidence exists, the prior Beneficiary's position is unchanged, except that a transferor who has received the agreed consideration under the designating operation — as evidenced by that operation or by the records produced under this Section 7.3(c) — holds their position subject to the consummated disposition and shall not exercise Owner Rights inconsistently with it. A person named without their assent may disregard the designation, and no act of theirs is required to reject it.
      6. **Scope; security.** A Qualified Designation cannot arise from mere custody: deposits, escrows, bridges, collateralization by a Beneficiary for the Beneficiary's own account (including any borrowing in which the Beneficiary is designated as their own owner of record, which effects no change of beneficial ownership), and every other Functional Contract operation not satisfying clause 1 retain exactly the treatment stated in **Section 7.3(b)**. A designation contained in, or resulting from, a Transfer or operation that itself resulted from unauthorized access to, or unauthorized use of, any Account or Smart Wallet is governed by **Section 7.5**, and a Functional Contract's records cannot validate what **Section 7.5.1** invalidates; the rights of qualifying purchasers and protected secured parties under **Section 11.4**, and of good-faith reliant parties under **Section 4.3**, are unaffected. This Section 7.3(c) is defined functionally and applies to any Functional Contract whose operation satisfies its terms; it does not depend on, or confer any status upon, any particular platform, protocol, or vendor.

   The distinction between custody and beneficial ownership ensures that temporary token operations through Functional Contracts do not unintentionally affect the underlying property rights, while preserving the flexibility to use smart contract infrastructure for property management and transfer.

7. **Acceptance and Disclaimer of Unsolicited Transfers.**
   1. **Presumptive defeasible vesting.** Consistent with **Sections 7.1 and 7.2**, beneficial ownership vests in the transferee upon a Transfer of the Deed Token to the transferee's Account or Smart Wallet without any act of acceptance, and this Section adds no acceptance step to any Transfer. In the case of a Transfer that the transferee did not solicit, initiate, or agree to acquire (an "**unsolicited Transfer**"), the vesting is defeasible by disclaimer as provided in this Section. Holding the Deed Token empowers the transferee immediately — all of the Owner Rights are exercisable from the moment of receipt — and imposes no obligation that survives a valid Onchain Disclaimer other than, where the disclaimer is made by recorded refusal, the limited custodial duties stated in **Section 7.7.2(c)**: pending acceptance or disclaimer, the presumptive transferee bears the incidents of ownership presumptively, as **Sections 6.1 and 12.7** provide, subject to retroactive divestment by a valid Onchain Disclaimer under clause 3. No incident of ownership becomes irrevocable except by the transferee's acceptance as provided in clause 4. The disclaimer right does not lapse by the passage of time, by silence, or by the transferee's ignorance of the Transfer; it ends only as provided in clause 4 or by operation of mandatory law. A transferee asserting an Onchain Disclaimer bears the burden of establishing that the Transfer was unsolicited; a Transfer shown by any signed record or onchain act to have been bargained for or agreed to is conclusively solicited.
   2. **Onchain Disclaimer.** A transferee of an unsolicited Transfer who has not accepted the Beneficial Interest under clause 4 may refuse the Beneficial Interest in whole (an "**Onchain Disclaimer**") by any of the following, each of which the parties designate as a signed record delivered to the Trustee and the transferor by its recording on the Blockchain Network, no offchain instrument, filing, or further delivery being required:
      - (a) **Return.** A Confirmed Transaction transferring the Deed Token back to the Address from which it was received, or to another Account or Smart Wallet controlled by the transferor (where a transferor-controlled destination is identifiable, it is the preferred destination). This is the ordinary disclaimer. A good-faith return under this clause (a) is not an unauthorized Transfer by, and creates no liability of, the disclaimant to the true Beneficiary or any other person, even where the sending Address proves not to have been the true Beneficiary's — the remedies of **Section 7.5** run against the wrongdoer; and the disclaimant's selection among the receiving Address and other transferor-controlled destinations is a mode of return, not a direction of disposition within the meaning of clause 3.
      - (b) **Disclaimer Burn.** A Redeem of the Deed Token executed before any acceptance under clause 4 and not in connection with any redemption demand, direction to the Trustee, or other exercise of Owner Rights (a "**Disclaimer Burn**"). A Disclaimer Burn is an irrevocable refusal of the whole Beneficial Interest; it is not an exercise of Owner Rights and is not a Dissolution Event or an instruction to convey under **Section 10.1**. Upon a Disclaimer Burn, the transferor (or the transferor's successor) is the continuing Beneficiary of a Trust whose Deed Token has been destroyed, and may restore control of the Property and effect conveyance through **Section 10.2** (as modified by **Section 10.2.7**) or **Section 10.4**. Whether a Redeem was executed in connection with a redemption demand, direction, or other exercise of Owner Rights is determined by the signed records satisfying **Section 4.1**: production of a signed demand or direction made at or before the Redeem conclusively establishes the Redeem as an exercise of Owner Rights under **Sections 7.7.4 and 10.1**, and not a Disclaimer Burn.
      - (c) **Recorded refusal (fallback).** Only where both return and Redemption are impossible, are prohibited by applicable law, or are not reasonably available to the transferee, a Confirmed Transaction originated by the transferee's Account or Smart Wallet that records, in a form retrievable from the Blockchain Network and logically associated with the Deed Token, the transferee's unequivocal refusal of the whole Beneficial Interest. A disclaimant proceeding under this clause (c) who continues to hold the Deed Token holds it thereafter as a mere custodian for the rightful Beneficiary, without Owner Rights and without duties other than to refrain from dealing with the Deed Token and to permit and reasonably cooperate with its retrieval; any later purported exercise or Transfer by the disclaimant is unauthorized and governed by **Sections 7.5 and 11.4**.

      An Onchain Disclaimer must refuse the entire Beneficial Interest received in the disclaimed Transfer; no partial disclaimer is permitted. Nothing in this Section precludes a disclaimant from also making a disclaimer satisfying the form, content, filing, or delivery requirements of an applicable statutory disclaimer regime; the parties intend an Onchain Disclaimer to operate as such a disclaimer to the maximum extent applicable law permits.
   3. **Relation back.** An Onchain Disclaimer is effective retroactively to the moment of the disclaimed Transfer, under this Agreement and to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law: the Beneficial Interest is treated as never having vested in the disclaimant; the disclaimant is treated as never having been a Beneficiary or Trustee; and the incidents of ownership arising under this Agreement — including the responsibilities and entitlements of **Section 6.1** and the tax attribution of **Section 12.7** — are attributed as if the disclaimed Transfer had not occurred, so that the transferor (or, if the transferor has died, the transferor's successors under **Section 7.6**) remained the Beneficiary throughout. The transferor's succession arrangements under **Section 7.6** are unaffected by the disclaimed Transfer. An Onchain Disclaimer is a refusal to accept; it is not a transfer, assignment, or release of the Beneficial Interest by the disclaimant, and the disclaimant may not direct its destination. Relation back does not discharge the limited custodial duties stated in **Section 7.7.2(c)**: a disclaimant by recorded refusal who continues to hold the Deed Token remains bound, for as long as that holding continues, to refrain from dealing with the Deed Token and to permit and reasonably cooperate with its retrieval, notwithstanding that the disclaimant is treated as never having been a Beneficiary or Trustee; those duties are the only obligations under this Agreement that survive a valid Onchain Disclaimer. This relation back binds every person bound by this Agreement; as against persons not so bound it operates to the extent applicable law gives it effect, and it is in every case subject to mandatory law — including federal and state tax and tax-collection law, any statutory disclaimer regime of applicable law that conditions a disclaimer's effect on its being made within a reasonable or prescribed time (in California, Cal. Prob. Code § 279), sanctions law, judicial sales, insolvency and creditor law, and the finality of instruments recorded in the real property records — and to **Sections 4.3 and 11.4**. Relation back does not extend or revive any statutory refund, appeal, or limitation period.
   4. **Acceptance; conclusive and retroactive to receipt.** The disclaimer right terminates upon the transferee's acceptance of the Beneficial Interest, which occurs upon the earlier of: (a) any voluntary exercise of Owner Rights by the transferee — including any Confirmed Transaction originated by the transferee's Account or Smart Wallet that transfers, lists, encumbers, lends against, fractionalizes, or otherwise deals with the Deed Token; any direction to the Trustee; any redemption demand, including a Redeem executed in connection with a demand or direction that the Trustee convey the Property, which is acceptance and preserves in full the transferee's right to conveyance under **Section 10.1**; and any other exercise of rights under this Agreement — other than an act described in clause 2, an act reasonably necessary to preserve the Deed Token or the Property pending refusal, or a listing or offer that is cancelled or expires without resulting in any Transfer or other consummated dealing, which is not acceptance, the disclaimer right subsisting notwithstanding it (a listing that results in a Transfer is acceptance, and **Section 4.3.2** protects any person who in good faith and for value relied on the listing); or (b) the transferee's express acceptance by Confirmed Transaction or other signed record satisfying **Section 4.1**. Acceptance is conclusive and confirms the vesting described in clause 1 retroactively to the moment of the Transfer: the transferee is, and is treated as having been from receipt, the Beneficiary for all purposes of this Agreement. An acceptance evidenced by a Confirmed Transaction is conclusive as to all persons. Conduct occurring offchain — including taking possession or use of the Property or receiving or directing its income — is likewise acceptance, but as against a person acting in good faith on the record of the Blockchain Network, **Section 4.3** governs, and acceptance and disclaimer status may be determined by such a person from the record of the Blockchain Network alone. A Transfer that the transferee solicited, initiated, or agreed to acquire — including every purchase, exchange, settlement, Qualified Designation under **Section 7.3(c)**, and other bargained-for acquisition — is accepted upon the Transfer itself, and no disclaimer right exists with respect to it.
   5. **Coordination; limits.** The following limits and coordinating rules apply:

   1. **The Deed Token.** A qualifying purchaser acquires its rights in the Deed Token free of a claim of a property right in the Deed Token, as UCC § 12-104(e) provides.    5. **Allocation.** Where a qualifying purchaser or protected secured party prevails, the prior Beneficiary's remedy is against the party who effected the unauthorized Transfer, not against the qualifying purchaser or protected secured party. This allocation is deliberate: it preserves the prior holder's ownership against thieves, donees, and transferees with notice (**Section 7.5.1**), while giving good-faith purchasers for value the finality that makes the Deed Token reliable in commerce; as between the two innocent parties, the loss falls on the holder responsible for the security of the compromised credentials (**Section 7.5.2**), whose recourse is against the wrongdoer. Third-party finality under this Section rests on applicable law and on the property and trust rules stated here, not on **Section 4.1** alone; whether a purchaser or secured party met these conditions remains subject to adjudication under **Sections 7.5.4 and 12.4**.

Knowledge or constructive notice of an interest in the Property appearing in the real property records — including any recorded mortgage, lien, lis pendens, or tax lien — is not notice of an Adverse Claim; such interests bind the Trust's title under **Section 11.8** and are not claims to the Deed Token or the Beneficial Interest. 8. **Priority Framework: the Land Lane and the Token Lane.** Interests in the Property, on the one hand, and interests in the Deed Token and the Beneficial Interest, on the other, arise and travel in two distinct legal lanes. This Section states the framework — which restates, builds on, and in no event weakens **Section 11.6** — by which this Agreement allocates every interaction between them:
   1. **The land lane.** As to record title to, and interests in, the Property itself, the real-property law of the jurisdiction where the Property is located — including its recording acts, lien-priority rules, tax-lien and tax-sale law, foreclosure procedures, and homestead laws — governs, always.    2. **The token lane.** As to ownership, assignment, encumbrance, and priority in the Deed Token and the Beneficial Interest, this Agreement, applicable trust law, and — to the extent applicable — UCC Articles 9 and 12 govern. The Beneficiary's interest is at all times an interest in the Trust (**Section 1.4**): the token lane controls everything the Trust has; the land lane determines what the Trust has.
   3. **The crossing gate.** The event that carries a land-lane outcome into the token lane is a recorded instrument. When an instrument effecting a transfer of the Trust's title by operation of law or legal process is recorded, **Section 10.5** converts the token-side position accordingly: the Trust dissolves as to the Property, the Deed Token becomes a Void Token, and the token-side claim converts to the wind-up authority and proceeds entitlement of **Section 10.5.2** and the reinstatement eligibility of **Section 10.5.4**. Where the recorded conveyance deed recites the Deed Contract address and Deed ID as **Section 3.1.2** requires, that deed gives such notice of the Trust and of the deed-property relationship as the recording acts of the jurisdiction give to its recitals; no additional recording is required for the Trust's title to be of record.
   4. **Applications.** Without limiting the foregoing: (a) a lien or encumbrance validly recorded against the Property has priority, as to the Property, over any later token-side pledge or Transfer; the later pledgee or transferee acquires the Beneficial Interest, or a security interest in the Deed Token or Beneficial Interest, in a Trust whose title is so encumbered, and if the recorded lien is foreclosed and the resulting instrument recorded, the token side holds the **Section 10.5.2** rights in any surplus; (b) a purchaser of the Deed Token acquires the Beneficial Interest in a Trust whose title is exactly what the land records and applicable real-property law make it — the protections of **Section 11.4** run against competing claims to the Deed Token and the Beneficial Interest, never against interests in the Property; and (c) whether an unrecorded land-side claim binds the Trust's title is determined by the recording acts and other applicable real-property law, which this Agreement neither strengthens nor weakens.
   5. **Court residual.** Judicial resolution under **Sections 10.4 and 12.4** remains available for the cases this framework does not answer mechanically, including: competing claims to beneficial ownership of the Deed Token itself (**Section 7.5.4**); contests recorded during a Quiet Period (**Section 10.2.2**); challenges to the validity of an Operative Instrument (**Section 10.5.3**); disputes concerning assent to a designation (**Section 7.3(c)**), solicitation or disclaimer of a Transfer (**Section 7.7**), or death and successor identity (**Sections 7.6 and 10.3**); and the status of the Deed Token under **Section 11.1**.
9. **Governing Law of the Deed Token; Fallback Without Article 12.**
   1. Matters governed by UCC Article 12 are governed by the local law of the Deed Token's jurisdiction, which **Section 11.1** expressly designates as the State of California pursuant to UCC § 12-107(c)(1); under UCC § 12-107(e), that law applies even if a matter or transaction bears no relation to California. The location of the Property therefore does not determine the law governing the Deed Token: matters of real-property law are governed by the mandatory law of the situs (**Sections 11.6 and 12.3**); matters concerning the Deed Token as a record are not.

The assignment mechanics of this Agreement and applicable trust law (**Sections 1.4 and 7**) do not depend on UCC Article 12: they continue to vest the Beneficial Interest in the person determined under **Section 7** upon each valid transfer of beneficial ownership, binding every person who claims under or through this Agreement or the Deed Token.    3. Custodial holding as described in **Section 11.3** remains available but is never required; nothing in this Section requires the use of a custodian or any other third-party arrangement for the Deed Token to carry the Beneficial Interest.
